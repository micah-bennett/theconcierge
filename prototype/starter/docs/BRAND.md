# HOP brand rules for the Portal

- **Name:** HOP, Hospitality On-Site Professionals. HOP is a standalone brand.
- **Brand line:** Radical Hospitality for the people who keep healthcare running.
- **Promise:** You take care of everyone else. We take care of the errands.
- **Audience:** nurses, doctors, and every healthcare professional, in and out of the hospital.
- **Voice:** warm, concise, best friend energy. Always there in the clutch. No slang that could offend, no emojis in marketing copy (icons inside the app are fine).

## Colors

| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Navy | #012f6c | Headlines, primary dark buttons, header |
| Teal | #04a6a8 | Primary action buttons, progress, success |
| Sky | #4ea1e3 | Secondary accents, info |
| Cream | #f7f4ee | App screen background |
| Gold | #e8a93a | Rewards, streaks, warnings |
| Coral | #e8625a | Clutch, alerts, high strain |

## Type

- Display: Montserrat 700 to 800
- Body: DM Sans 400 to 700

All tokens live in `app/globals.css` (`:root`) and `lib/brand.ts`. Change them in one place.

## Status labels

New offer, Accepted, Started, In progress, Completed, Needs attention. Use these exact words everywhere.

## Never

- Never state prices, signed clients or partners, or that the Portal is live until it is.
- Never show an individual's health data to a hospital.
